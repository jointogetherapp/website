A LITTLE STRUCTURE. A LOT LESS CHASING.

From “who’s in?”
to “see you there.”

One simple rhythm for the gathering you want to keep going.

01

Make the plan clear.

Pick the gathering, capacity, minimum turnout, and a decision deadline. Everyone can see what the plan needs before they reply.

WEDNESDAY GAME NIGHT6 seats · 4 people needed

Decision deadline: Tuesday at 6:00 PM
Gathering: Wednesday at 7:00 PM

02

Get a real reply.

Members confirm or pass. If the gathering is full, they can join standby. A maybe isn’t counted as a confirmed seat.

ONE SHARED HEADCOUNT5 confirmed. Minimum met.

A readable status, without scrolling through a conversation.

03

Give open seats a next step.

A cancellation creates an offer for the next person on standby. They accept to become confirmed. If they decline or the offer expires, the next person gets a turn.

NO SILENT PROMOTIONSOffered ≠ confirmed.

A pending offer never counts toward your minimum turnout.

04

Know where things stand.

At the deadline, the confirmed count determines whether the minimum is met. If a later cancellation takes you below it, that change stays visible. The organizer can decide the next step.

AN HONEST STATUSBelow minimum? Say so.

A tool can clarify the plan. It can’t guarantee attendance.
