A clear note
about your data.

This is a local product preview, not a live social service. Updated 3 October 2026.

What this preview stores.

Your chosen nickname, avatar, introduction, sample circle, reading preferences, start date, pace, chapter progress, local posts, and votes are stored in your browser’s localStorage. They are not an account and do not sync across devices.

Use fictional or non-identifying details. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to see what you save. Browser storage is not a private vault.

What it doesn’t send.

The application does not send your preview profile, posts, progress, or votes to Together, other readers, an AI provider, or a payment provider. There is no live AI, email signup, payment collection, analytics script, or advertising tracker in this build.

Hosting and outside links.

If you visit a hosted copy, its hosting provider may process normal request data such as IP addresses and access logs. External book links open Project Gutenberg, whose own policies apply. No book text is copied into this preview.

You’re in control.

Open the Me tab to export your local preview as a JSON file, or reset it. Reset removes this preview’s stored data from this browser only. It cannot remove exports you have already downloaded. Clearing browser data also removes saved progress.

Before live launch.

A live service will need an identified operator and contact channel, a complete privacy notice, account deletion and data-access workflows, appropriate security and retention rules, and clear AI and payment disclosures. Those features are not enabled by this preview.
