THE IDEA BEHIND TOGETHER

It starts with
someone saying, “Again?”

A game that runs late. A book that gets everyone talking. A familiar table and a Wednesday that suddenly feels like the best part of the week.

Then someone has to make next time happen.

Who’s coming? Do we have enough people? Is that open seat actually taken? It’s ordinary, invisible work. And too often, it lands on the same person.

Make the plan lighter.
Keep the people close.

That’s what we’re building Together for: the small, established groups that want to keep gathering. Clear replies. A minimum headcount. A thoughtful way to offer an open seat.

We aren’t trying to replace the friendships, the conversation, or the person who brings everyone together. We want to give the practical details a better home.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

Together is an early product in development. Our public demo is a sandbox, not a live group service. It’s a way to make the idea tangible while we build carefully.
