A little care goes a long way

Good conversations
begin with care.

Different takes are welcome. Disrespect isn’t. These are the principles we want every Together circle to share.

Make space for one another.

Be curious. Let quieter voices in. Disagree with an idea without attacking the person behind it. Harassment, threats, hate, unwanted sexual attention, and pressure to disclose personal details have no place here.

Keep the surprise in the story.

Post in the right chapter thread. Stay within that chapter, even when you’ve read ahead. The preview hides threads beyond your saved progress by default; you can explicitly open one if you choose.

Meet thoughtfully.

For any future in-person circle, choose a busy public venue, make your own travel arrangements, tell someone you trust where you’re going, and feel free to leave. Never publish a home address. Together has not verified or booked any venue in this preview.

Privacy is part of belonging.

A nickname is enough. Don’t share someone else’s contact details, photos, messages, or identity without their permission. Never post sensitive information in this local preview.

Before a live community opens.

Reporting, blocking, moderator review, host responsibilities, and abuse response must be in place and tested. Those systems are not active here. This preview is not monitored, and local posts are not sent to a team.

For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. This app cannot provide emergency help.

An adult community.

The planned initial service is for adults aged 18 and over. This preview does not verify age or create accounts.
