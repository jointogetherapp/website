A few good questions.

Is this a live community?

Not yet. This is a functional product preview. You can explore a sample circle, save reading progress, write local chapter posts, and try next-book voting. Nothing is shared with other readers.

Will everyone in my circle need Plus?

The proposed model is one paying creator, with friends joining that circle for free. The offer is not active, and no subscription can be purchased here.

What does “extra AI prompts” mean?

Plus is intended to include a clear monthly allowance, with limits to control cost and keep the circle human-led. The allowance is not final. This preview uses curated questions and makes no AI requests.

Do I get the books?

The preview links to free public-domain originals at Project Gutenberg. Availability can vary by country. Future copyrighted books would need to be sourced lawfully by readers or through licensed partnerships.

Does in-person cost more?

No additional Together in-person fee is proposed. Any future venue, travel, or refreshment costs would need to be made clear separately. This preview has no booked venue or event.
