A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
pull up a chair.

Can I use Together with my group today?

Not yet. You can explore the working, simulated demo now. Real group accounts, invitations, and recurring gatherings are still in development.

Is this another group chat?

No. The planned product focuses on responses, headcounts, and standby places. Keep your existing chat for the conversation.

Who is it for?

Our starting point is adult game-night and social groups: roughly 12–30 people in the group, with 4–8 seats at a gathering. The demo uses a six-seat game night.

What will it cost?

The planned organizer price is $8 USD per month or $72 USD per year, with up to 30 group members. Members join free. These are proposed prices, not a live paid offer. See the planned offer.
